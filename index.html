Netflix: How to Watch TV Shows and Movies Online

Plans, pricing, devices, and setup — explained simply for new and existing subscribers.

Netflix is one of the most widely used streaming services in the world, giving subscribers access to a large library of TV shows, movies, documentaries, and original productions that can be watched on demand. Whether you're setting up an account for the first time or trying to get more out of an existing subscription, this guide walks through how Netflix works, what it costs, how to watch on different devices, and answers to the questions people search for most often.

What Is Netflix?

Netflix is a subscription-based streaming platform that lets members watch TV series, films, and original content over the internet, without needing cable or satellite service. Instead of paying per show or renting individual titles, subscribers pay a recurring monthly fee for unlimited access to whatever is included in their plan's library.

Direct answer: Netflix works by streaming video content over an internet connection to phones, tablets, computers, smart TVs, and streaming devices, using a monthly subscription rather than pay-per-view pricing.

How Does Netflix Work?

Netflix doesn't require downloading physical media or scheduling around broadcast times. Content is delivered on demand, meaning you choose what to watch and when. The platform also uses viewing history to suggest shows and movies that match your habits, and it supports multiple user profiles so people sharing an account can get individually tailored recommendations.

Key features worth knowing

  • On-demand streaming — start, pause, or resume any title at any time
  • Multiple profiles — separate recommendations and watch history for each household member
  • Downloads for offline viewing — save select titles to watch without an internet connection
  • Multiple device support — switch between phone, tablet, laptop, and TV seamlessly
  • Parental controls — set content restrictions for kids' profiles

Netflix Plans and Pricing (What to Expect)

Netflix typically offers a few subscription tiers that differ by video quality, number of simultaneous screens, and whether ads are included. Exact pricing varies by country and changes periodically, so it's worth checking the official Netflix pricing page for current numbers in your region. In general, plans are structured around:

  • A lower-cost, ad-supported tier with limited download options
  • A standard tier without ads, usually supporting HD streaming on more screens
  • A premium tier with the highest video quality (often Ultra HD) and the most simultaneous streams
Direct answer: Netflix pricing is tiered by video quality and number of screens, with an ad-supported option generally being the cheapest and premium 4K plans being the most expensive.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Start Watching Netflix Online

  1. Go to the official Netflix website or app — Visit netflix.com or download the Netflix app from your device's app store (available for iOS, Android, smart TVs, and gaming consoles).
  2. Choose a plan — Select the subscription tier that fits your budget and how you plan to watch, such as how many screens you need or whether ads are acceptable.
  3. Create an account — Enter your email address and set a password. Netflix will ask for basic account details to get you started.
  4. Enter payment information — Add a valid payment method. Netflix may offer a trial period in some regions, though free trials have become less common over time.
  5. Set up your profile — Create a profile with your name and preferences. If multiple people will use the account, add separate profiles for each person, including a Kids profile if needed.
  6. Browse and start watching — Use the search bar or browse curated rows by genre, popularity, or "Because you watched…" recommendations to find something to watch.
  7. Adjust playback and download settings — Go to account settings to manage video quality, download preferences, and notification options based on your internet connection and storage.
  8. Watch on your preferred device — Log into the same account on your phone, smart TV, tablet, or computer to continue watching across devices.

Devices You Can Use to Watch Netflix

Netflix is built to work across a wide range of devices, including:

  • Smart TVs (most major brands)
  • Streaming devices (media players and streaming sticks)
  • Gaming consoles
  • Smartphones and tablets (iOS and Android)
  • Web browsers on desktop or laptop computers
Direct answer: You can watch Netflix on nearly any internet-connected screen, including smart TVs, phones, tablets, computers, and streaming devices, by logging into the Netflix app or website with your account.

Tips for a Better Netflix Viewing Experience

  • Check your internet speed — smoother streaming and higher resolution require a stable connection; slower connections may need a lower video quality setting
  • Use profiles wisely — separate profiles keep recommendations accurate for each viewer
  • Download before travel — save shows or movies in advance if you expect limited or no internet access
  • Review your plan periodically — your ideal tier may change based on how many people use the account and what video quality you need
  • Enable parental controls — set maturity ratings and profile locks for children's profiles

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Netflix available worldwide?

Netflix is available in most countries, though the specific shows and movies offered can vary by region due to licensing agreements.

Do I need a fast internet connection to watch Netflix?

A stable broadband connection is recommended for smooth streaming. Higher video quality, such as HD or Ultra HD, generally requires a faster connection than standard definition.

Can I watch Netflix without an internet connection?

Yes, in a limited way. Many titles can be downloaded in advance on the Netflix mobile app and watched offline, though this isn't available for every title.

How many people can use one Netflix account?

This depends on your subscription plan, since plans differ by how many simultaneous streams and profiles they support. Some plans also have rules around sharing an account outside your household.

Can I cancel my Netflix subscription anytime?

Most Netflix subscriptions can be canceled at any time through account settings, and access typically continues until the end of the current billing period.

Does Netflix have both TV shows and movies?

Yes. Netflix offers a mix of licensed TV shows and films along with Netflix Original series and movies produced specifically for the platform.

Is there a free version of Netflix?

Netflix does not generally offer a permanent free tier, though promotional trials have been offered in some markets at various times. Availability changes, so it's best to check current offers directly on Netflix.

Conclusion

Netflix remains a popular way to watch TV shows and movies online because it combines a large content library with flexible, on-demand access across almost any device. Understanding the available plans, setup steps, and basic features can help you get the most value out of a subscription, whether you're a new user or looking to fine-tune how you watch.
